# Disclaimer

## The snippet used in the app

> Facts-only. No investment advice.

That is the exact string the interface shows on load, taken from
`config.DISCLAIMER` in `src/config.py`. It is a fixed constant, never
generated by the language model.

## What it means in practice

The assistant answers published facts about the five HDFC Mutual Fund
schemes named in `SOURCES.md`, and every factual answer carries one link
to the page the fact was read from plus a `Last updated from sources:`
line dated from when that page was fetched.

It does not, and by design cannot:

- recommend buying, selling, holding, or switching a scheme;
- calculate, compare, rank, or quote returns or performance figures;
- answer about a fund, AMC, or asset class outside the five sources;
- accept, store, or echo personal identifiers such as PAN, Aadhaar,
  account numbers, OTPs, email addresses, or phone numbers.

Where a question needs a return figure, the app redirects to the
official factsheet published by the AMC rather than producing a number.
Where retrieval finds nothing relevant, it says the information is not
in its sources instead of guessing.

Nothing here is investment advice. Mutual fund investments are subject to
market risks; read the scheme's own documents and speak to a registered
mutual fund distributor before acting.
